# ENSIR Child Safety Standards

**Last updated: September 9, 2026**

ENSIR is committed to protecting users from child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE).

## Age Restriction

ENSIR is intended only for users aged 18 or older. Minors are not permitted to create or use an ENSIR account.

## Prohibited Conduct

ENSIR strictly prohibits:

* Child sexual abuse material (CSAM)
* Sexual exploitation or grooming of minors
* Content that sexualizes or endangers children
* Attempts to contact minors for sexual purposes
* Sharing, requesting, promoting, or distributing CSAM

Accounts associated with such conduct may be suspended or permanently removed. Where required, relevant information may be reported to the appropriate authorities.

## Reporting Safety Concerns

Users can report suspected abuse, exploitation, inappropriate conduct, or safety concerns through the reporting tools available within the ENSIR application.

## Cooperation With Authorities

ENSIR complies with applicable child-safety laws and cooperates with law-enforcement authorities and relevant child-protection organizations when legally required.
